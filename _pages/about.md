Welcome to my website! 
I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Political Science at the University of Pittsburgh. My main research interests are American political behavior and Political Methodology.

My research examines inequality, representation, and political attitudes. My current work focuses on how people's social and economic experiences shape their views of opportunity, fairness, and the political system.

My broader interests include political attitudes, public opinion, and Bayesian methods.

Please feel free to reach out！
