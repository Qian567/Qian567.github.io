## Working Papers

**Intergenerational Social Mobility, Racial Attitude, and Anti-Immigrant Sentiment**

*Presented at MPSA 2025.*

This project examines the relationship between intergenerational social mobility, racial attitudes, and anti-immigrant sentiment in the United States.

**Intergenerational Educational Mobility and Meritocracy Beliefs**

*With Angie N. Ocampo-Roland.*

This project investigates how intergenerational educational mobility is associated with beliefs in meritocracy and the American Dream, with particular attention to differences across racial and ethnic groups.
